Straight answers

What people ask before booking a clean

Including the one that decides whether your roof survives the process.

Will pressure washing damage my roof?

It can, badly. High pressure strips the granular surface off concrete tiles and shortens the life of the roof considerably. We scrape and treat rather than blast. If somebody offers to jet wash your roof cheaply, that is why it is cheap.

How long does a clean last?

Depends on the aspect and the trees around you. North-facing and shaded roofs go green fastest. A biocide treatment after cleaning slows the regrowth substantially, and a protective coating on render slows it further still.

Do I need scaffolding?

Usually not for a roof clean — most jobs are done from a ladder or from the ground. If the property or the pitch means we do need access equipment we will say so in the quote rather than adding it later.

What is ProperLa?

A breathable protective coating for walls and render. Water runs off instead of soaking in, so algae and moss have nothing to grow on and the wall stays drier. We are approved applicators. It goes on after cleaning, never instead of it.

Can you do the gutters and driveway at the same time?

Yes, and it is cheaper that way because the access only has to be set up once. Worth asking for a price on the lot even if you only think you need one of them.
